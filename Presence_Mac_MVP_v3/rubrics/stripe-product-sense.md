# Stripe Product Sense Rubric

Stripe Product Sense contributes 50% of the overall interview score. Delivery and Language contributes the other 50%. The four Stripe dimensions total 50 raw points and are normalized to 100 in the report.

| Dimension | Points | Strong performance |
|---|---:|---|
| User empathy | 14 | Identifies meaningful customer segments, distinguishes functional and emotional needs, prioritizes a segment and pain point, and ties recommendations to those needs. |
| Product strategy | 14 | Establishes company mission, macro and business goals, investment rationale, competitive context, differentiation, and well-reasoned success metrics before focusing on features. |
| Product design | 14 | Generates multiple creative solutions, describes the high-level user flow, selects an MVP with explicit rationale, and addresses technical, design, and market constraints, trade-offs, and edge cases. |
| Critical thinking and judgment | 8 | Structures ambiguity, states assumptions, separates important considerations from distractions, thinks aloud, makes defensible decisions, and adapts to follow-ups. |

## Interview behavior

The interview is collaborative and has no single correct answer. The interviewer should challenge assumptions, probe decisions, and offer only a small directional nudge when the candidate is genuinely stuck.

In the three-products scenario, the candidate names and assesses three products. The interviewer chooses one and supplies an unexpected new user segment or use case. All later questions stay within that product adaptation.

## Evidence standard

Every score requires evidence from the candidate's spoken answer or shared notes. Missing reasoning is not inferred. Critical Thinking measures decision quality and handling of ambiguity; verbal clarity is scored separately under Delivery and Language.
