# Product Sense Rubric

Product Sense is 50% of the overall score. These dimensions total 50 raw points and are normalized to 100.

| Dimension | Points | Strong performance |
|---|---:|---|
| Product motivation | 8 | Clarifies the objective and connects user value to product/business purpose. |
| Segmentation | 12 | Maps the ecosystem, segments by behavior or motivation, and explains the target choice. |
| Problem identification | 13 | Builds a journey and prioritizes specific pain points by severity and frequency. |
| Solution development | 13 | Creates distinct options, prioritizes trade-offs, and defines V1, metrics, risks, and GTM where relevant. |
| Synthesis and judgment | 4 | Maintains a coherent narrative, summarizes decisions, and adapts to follow-ups. |

## Core-section floor

If Product motivation, Segmentation, Problem identification, or Solution development is below 60%, Product Sense cannot exceed 69/100.

Every score requires evidence from speech or notes. Missing evidence is scored as missing. Notes support structure and prioritization; they are not a writing contest.

Adapted for Presence from the practices in Lenny Rachitsky's *The definitive guide to mastering the product management case interview*.
