# Delivery and Language Rubric

Delivery and Language is 50% of the overall score. These dimensions total 50 raw points and are normalized to 100.

| Dimension | Points | Strong performance |
|---|---:|---|
| Structure and waypointing | 12 | Gives a game plan, signposts transitions, and synthesizes. |
| Clarity and executive communication | 10 | Leads with decisions and supports them concisely. |
| Professional conversational fluency | 8 | Sounds collaborative, handles interruptions, and uses calm pauses. |
| Grammar | 8 | Uses precise grammar; feedback tracks repeated patterns with examples and corrections. |
| Fillers and verbal habits | 6 | Uses fillers sparingly and replaces repeated habits with purposeful pauses. |
| Pronunciation and intelligibility | 6 | Is consistently understandable; only audio-supported patterns are flagged. |

Never penalize an accent or speculate about nationality, emotion, or competence. Pronunciation feedback requires usable audio and must be conservative. Coaching prioritizes repeated, high-impact patterns.

Presence stores compact session summaries in the browser on the user's Mac. Future judges receive recent weakness tags and scores, while each new interview is scored independently.
